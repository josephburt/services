Rates

Services & pricing

Clear starting prices. Parts, extra labor, and house-call premiums are confirmed before work begins.

Diagnostic

$65

A focused evaluation of the problem — hardware, software, or both. If you move forward with the repair, this fee is applied toward the work.

Tune-up / OS reinstall

$50–$150

Cleanup for a slow machine, or a clean operating-system install when that’s the better path. The range depends on the system and how much work it needs.

Laptop screen

$100 + parts

Labor to replace a cracked or failing display. The panel itself is quoted after the diagnostic so you’re paying for the correct part, not a generic one.

Software data recovery

$50/hr + $65 diagnostic

Software-level recovery of files from failing or unreadable drives. The diagnostic comes first so we know whether recovery is realistic before the hourly work starts.

House call

Premium over drop-off

On-site service is available throughout Ellis County at a premium to drop-off. The extra charge is discussed and agreed on before the diagnostic, so there are no surprises at the door.
